🎙️ Voice Training

Start Strong

First line should sound steady, deep, and confident:
“Sir, my first priority will be to stay calm and keep my men composed.”

Avoid rushing. Pause slightly after “Sir.”

Use Pauses for Power

After each main point (safety, morale, roles, recon, etc.), pause for half a second.

This shows control and confidence.

Tone Variation

Safety & morale → warm, reassuring tone.

Orders & actions → firm, commanding tone.

Practice Louder

Speak at a volume as if you’re addressing a group outdoors.

Don’t shout, but make sure every word carries weight.

💂 Body Language

Posture

Sit or stand straight, chest slightly out, shoulders back.

No slouching or leaning.

Eye Contact

If in interview → look at the officer directly.

If practicing → pick a point on the wall and “command” it.

Hand Gestures

Keep them controlled (e.g., small hand movement when saying “defensive position”).

Avoid over-gesturing or fidgeting.

Facial Expression

Calm but serious.

Small confident smile only when talking about morale.
